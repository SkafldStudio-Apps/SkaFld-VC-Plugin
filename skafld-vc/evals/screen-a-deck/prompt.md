Screen this company for me. We are an angel group writing $50k to $250k cheques into seed B2B software. Here is their deck:

# Lumen Ledger - seed deck (fictional, for evaluation only)

1. Problem: finance teams at 50-500 person software companies close the books in 9 days on average, mostly reconciling card and invoice data by hand.
2. Solution: Lumen Ledger reconciles card spend, invoices and bank feeds automatically and closes the month in 2 days.
3. Traction: 14 paying customers, $38k MRR, growing 11% month on month for 6 months; net revenue retention 118%.
4. Market: 90,000 companies in the segment in the US and UK; "a $40B market".
5. Business model: $2,700 average monthly contract; gross margin 78%.
6. Competition: "no direct competitors"; incumbents are spreadsheets and the accounting system's own tools.
7. Team: CEO, former controller at a public SaaS company (8 years); CTO, former staff engineer at a payments company (6 years).
8. Raise: $3M seed on a post-money SAFE with a $18M valuation cap; 20 months of runway.
9. Use of funds: 60% engineering, 25% go-to-market, 15% operations.
