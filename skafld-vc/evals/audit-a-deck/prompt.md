Pick this deck apart slide by slide: which claims are unsupported, which numbers do not reconcile, and what should I ask the founders on the call?

# Lumen Ledger - seed deck (fictional, for evaluation only)

1. Problem: finance teams close the books in 9 days on average.
2. Solution: Lumen Ledger closes the month in 2 days.
3. Traction: 14 paying customers, $38k MRR; "customers love us".
4. Market: "a $40B market".
5. Business model: $2,700 average monthly contract; gross margin 78%.
6. Competition: "no direct competitors".
7. Raise: $3M; 20 months of runway; hiring 12 engineers this year.
